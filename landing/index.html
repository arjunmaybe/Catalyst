Catalyst does format conversion only — no compress, crop, trim, redact, metadata, collage, or PDF tools.

Privacy

Your files stay on your PC.

Conversion runs locally — images through the Rust image crate, video and audio through a bundled ffmpeg binary. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

Uploading adds risk

Online converters make you upload a copy of your file, so you have to trust how another service stores, processes, and deletes it.

Catalyst works locally

Everything converts on your own PC. Your files are never sent to us or anyone else.
