What it does

Catalyst skips the browser tabs, upload queues, and sketchy converter sites. It stays out of the way until you need it.

01

Drop

Drag any supported image, video, or audio file onto the Catalyst nucleus on your desktop.

02

Pick

Valid target formats orbit out around the file. Hover and pick the one you want.

03

Done

Conversion runs locally and the finished file lands next to your original. That's it.

Supported formats

One widget for the formats you convert most.

Images

  • JPG
  • PNG
  • BMP
  • GIF

Video

  • MP4
  • MKV
  • MOV
  • WEBM

Audio

  • MP3
  • WAV
  • M4A
  • FLAC
